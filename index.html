▶ How does the Weiss Schwarz European Championship (WSEC) work?
This page is maintained by the Weiss Schwarz UK Community's WSEC Representatives, and tracks the real-time live standings for the 2026 edition of the WSEC UK League.

Standings update automatically as episode results are submitted throughout the season.

A player's total points on the global and shop leaderboards are calculated from their top 5 episode results. As players continue to participate in new WSEC episodes, they can replace their weaker results with stronger results in their top 5, and improve their overall points total.

Leaderboard guide:
  • Gold - Player currently in the running for the UK League Champion position. This player receives a direct invite to the WSEC Finals.
  • Green - Players currently in the running for their respective Shop League Champion positions. These players receive direct invites to the WSEC Finals. This invite can be passed down if the same player tops 2 or more Shop Leagues. Shops must complete 4 or more episodes for their Shop League Champion invite to be valid.
  • Blue - Players currently 2nd or 3rd in their respective Shop Leagues. These players qualify for the post-season Top 24 Qualifier Playoffs, which can yield invites to the WSEC Finals. Invites to the Playoffs do not pass down.
  • Silver - Players who have received their WSEC Finals invites through other events.
Players who are confirmed to have qualified will be furnished with an icon next to their name.
